1. What we collect

When you join our waitlist or create an AdMagic account, we collect:

  • Your email address and name
  • Company name and role (optional, if you provide it)
  • Creative assets you upload for analysis (video files, static images)
  • Usage data — which features you use and how often
  • Technical data — browser type, device type, IP address (for security)

2. How we use your data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver the AdMagic service and analyze your uploaded creative assets
  • Send you product updates, early access invitations, and onboarding emails
  • Improve our AI models and platform features
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents

We will never use your uploaded creative assets to train general-purpose AI models without your explicit consent.

3. Who we share data with

We work with a small number of trusted service providers:

  • Loops.so — for email communications
  • Vercel — for hosting and infrastructure
  • Stripe — for payment processing (when billing is active)
  • Analytics providers — for aggregate usage statistics

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes. Period.

4. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Uploaded creative assets are retained for 30 days after processing unless you choose to save them to your account. You can request full deletion of your data at any time by emailing anmol@admagic.cloud.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at anmol@admagic.cloud.

6. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you logged in, and analytics cookies to understand how the product is used. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel only. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email anmol@admagic.cloud and we'll respond within 2 business days.
