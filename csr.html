Our commitments

We will never use your uploaded creative assets to train general-purpose AI models without your explicit, informed consent. Your data is yours.

We will publish an annual transparency report covering how our AI models work, what data they were trained on, and what steps we've taken to reduce bias.

We will offset 100% of our compute carbon footprint from day one. We track emissions per inference and report quarterly.

Ethical AI principles

Transparency

We believe you have a right to understand how AdMagic reaches its conclusions. Our analysis outputs explain which visual and audio elements drove each insight, so you can verify the reasoning — not just trust a black box.

No algorithmic discrimination

We actively test our models for demographic bias and will not ship an update that we know performs materially worse for any audience segment. If we discover bias, we fix it before re-deployment and disclose it in our transparency report.

Human oversight

AdMagic is a decision-support tool. We design every output to help marketers think better, not to automate decisions that should involve human judgment. High-stakes creative decisions belong with people.

Data minimisation

We collect the minimum data necessary to run the service. We do not build audience profiles, run tracking pixels, or sell insights about your users to third parties.

Environmental responsibility

Training and running AI models is energy-intensive. We take that seriously.

  • All production infrastructure runs on AWS regions powered by at least 80% renewable energy
  • We purchase verified carbon offsets for any residual emissions through Gold Standard certified projects
  • We optimise model inference to minimise compute per request — smaller footprint, faster results
  • We report our total cloud emissions publicly every quarter

Community & access

Sophisticated creative intelligence has historically been available only to large brands with big agencies and budgets. We're changing that.

  • 1% pledge: 1% of all revenue is donated to digital marketing literacy programmes in underserved communities across the Caribbean, West Africa, and South Asia — markets where our first customers operate.
  • Founder programme: We offer 90-day free access to qualified early-stage founders from underrepresented backgrounds. Apply at anmol@admagic.cloud.
  • Open research: We will publish academic papers on our creative intelligence methodology to advance the broader field.

Governance

AdMagic is a privately held company. Our co-founders are personally accountable for living up to these commitments. We will publish our first annual CSR report in Q2 2027, covering 2026 in full.
