**Brand assets used:** `flick-output/brand-assets/` — eclipse-banner.png, 1-4.jpg (PetPiano), heatmap-reference.png, design-before.jpg, design-after.png, competitor-ad-research.png (all real AdMagic product assets, no stock/placeholder art).

If you have real AdMagic numbers you want featured instead, tell me and I'll swap them in.
